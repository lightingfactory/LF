About the shop

A local lighting shop, stocked like a factory floor.

Lighting Factory carries fancy lights, hanging lights, wall lights, panel lights, strip lights, COB lights, flood lights and general electricals — for households, festive décor, and small businesses across Thrissur.

Walk in and browse the shelves, or send us a photo of the space you're lighting up over WhatsApp and we'll help you pick the right fitting. Pay & Parking is available just a short walk from the shop.

What we stock

Every kind of light, one shop.

Our range spans decorative and functional lighting — pick a category below or ask in-store for what's not listed here.

Fancy Lights

Decorative and festive lighting for celebrations, functions and accent lighting at home.

Hanging Lights

Pendant and drop fixtures for dining tables, entryways, staircases and double-height ceilings.

Wall Lights

Statement wall fittings and sconces that shape a room's mood, indoors and out.

Panel Lights

Slim ceiling panels for even, glare-free light across living rooms, offices and shops.

Strip Lights

Flexible LED strips for false ceilings, cove lighting, cabinets and accent trims.

COB Lights

Compact, high-output chip-on-board spotlights and downlights for focused illumination.

Flood Lights

Weatherproof, wide-beam lighting for building facades, yards, godowns and outdoor spaces.

LED & Electricals

Everyday LED bulbs, holders, and general electrical fittings for the home, shop or office.
